Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026 | Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Privacy Commitment: App Locker operates on a strict 100% on-device architecture. Passwords, intruder photos, and app lock lists never leave your device and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

This Privacy Policy applies to the App Locker & Intruder Security Vault mobile application developed by the App Locker Security Team.

1. Zero Remote Storage & Local Data Processing

We believe in absolute data privacy. All core security operations execute strictly locally on your Android device:

  • Intruder Photos: When an intruder enters an incorrect passcode, a front-camera snapshot is captured, immediately encrypted with hardware-backed AES-256 GCM, and saved to private sandboxed app storage. Intruder photos are never uploaded to any cloud server or third-party service.
  • Passcodes & Hash Digests: Passwords, PINs, and Pattern sequences are stored exclusively on-device in salted, encrypted format.
  • Biometric Data: Fingerprint and Face Unlock authentication is handled directly by Android's native BiometricPrompt API. The app never accesses, stores, or handles raw biometric templates.
  • Installed App Lists: Application metadata is queried locally to populate your app lock list and is never tracked, shared, or logged.

2. Required Android System Permissions & Purpose

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): Used exclusively to power the 0ms Instant Lock Engine by detecting window state transitions when a protected app is launched. We strictly do not read screen text, intercept user keystrokes, track user inputs, or collect personal data.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used as a fallback foreground monitoring mechanism when accessibility service is disabled.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables rendering the lock overlay interface immediately above protected apps.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely to capture silent intruder photos when failed unlock attempts exceed the user-defined threshold.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Authenticates the authorized user via device hardware sensors.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Maintains continuous background protection so apps remain locked reliably.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

Our app incorporates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google Play Billing:

  • Google AdMob: May collect anonymous or pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and analytics in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies. Developer verification file is hosted at app-ads.txt.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles secure purchase transactions for optional upgrades. Payment details are processed directly by Google Play.

4. Data Deletion & Multi-Pass Shredding

You maintain full control over your data. All intruder alerts and photos can be deleted at any time from within the app. Deletion triggers a 3-pass forensic shredder that overwrites physical storage sectors with random noise and zeros. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all encrypted logs from the device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
