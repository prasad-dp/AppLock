Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026 | Effective Date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the App Locker & Intruder Security Vault mobile application (Package ID: com.aistudio.applocker.kyzqpz) developed by the App Locker Security Team.

1. Zero Remote Storage & Local Data Processing

We believe in absolute data privacy. All core security operations execute strictly locally on your Android device:

  • Intruder Photos: When an intruder enters an incorrect passcode, a front-camera snapshot is captured, immediately encrypted with hardware-backed AES-256 GCM, and saved to private sandboxed app storage. Intruder photos are never uploaded to any cloud server, third-party database, or external service.
  • Passcodes & Hash Digests: Passwords, PINs, and Pattern sequences are stored exclusively on-device in salted, encrypted format.
  • Biometric Data: Fingerprint and Face Unlock authentication is handled directly by Android's native BiometricPrompt API. The app never accesses, stores, or handles raw biometric templates.
  • Installed App Lists: Application metadata is queried locally to populate your app lock list and is never tracked or shared.

2. Required Android System Permissions & Purpose

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): Used exclusively to power the 0ms Instant Lock Engine by detecting window state transitions (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED) when a protected app is launched. We do not read screen text, intercept user keystrokes, track user inputs, or collect personal data.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used by the fallback monitoring service to identify foreground application changes.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables the high-priority lock screen overlay above locked applications.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely to capture silent intruder photos when failed unlock attempts exceed the user-defined threshold.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Allows one-touch biometric authentication for the owner.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Ensures continuous, uninterrupted background protection.

4. Data Deletion & Multi-Pass Shredding

You maintain full control over your data. All intruder alerts and photos can be deleted at any time from the Intruder Logs tab. Deletion triggers a 3-pass forensic shredder that overwrites physical storage sectors with random noise and zeros. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all encrypted logs from the device.
