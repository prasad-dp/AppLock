Privacy Policy

Application: App Locker Pro & Intruder Security Vault (com.aistudio.applocker.kyzqpz)

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 | Last Updated: August 18, 2026

Privacy Commitment: App Locker Pro operates on a 100% on-device privacy architecture. All intruder photos, passwords, and app lock lists are encrypted locally and never transmitted to external cloud servers.

1. Data Handling & Cryptographic Storage

  • Silent Intruder Snapshots: Captured front-camera photos are encrypted immediately in memory with 256-bit AES GCM using hardware keys generated in the Android KeyStore. Encrypted .enc files reside strictly within isolated app sandboxed storage. Unencrypted raw photos are never written to physical disk.
  • Master Credentials: PINs, Patterns, and Passwords are saved on-device in salted, encrypted cryptographic format.
  • Biometric Credentials: Fingerprint and Face Unlock authentication leverages Android's official BiometricPrompt API. The app never accesses, logs, or transmits raw biometric data.

2. Android System Permissions & Play Store Policy Disclosure

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): Powers the 0ms Instant Lock Engine by detecting TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED events to lock target apps before window frames draw. We strictly do not read user screen contents, monitor typed text or keystrokes, track user actions, or transmit any data.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used as a fallback foreground monitoring mechanism when accessibility services are disabled.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables rendering the lock overlay interface immediately above protected apps.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively to silently capture intruder photos when consecutive incorrect passcode attempts exceed the user-defined limit.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Authenticates the authorized user via device hardware sensors.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Maintains continuous background protection so apps remain locked reliably.

3. Advertising & In-App Purchases

  • Google AdMob: Third-party ad SDK that may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and analytics. Pro subscribers enjoy an ad-free environment.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases securely through official Google Play infrastructure.

4. Data Deletion & Forensic Shredding

Users can permanently delete intruder alert logs and snapshots from within the app. Deleting records executes a 3-pass forensic shredder (overwriting physical sectors with random bytes and zeros before unlinking). Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local data.
